Our story

We are Comuna.

Comuna began with a familiar Los Angeles problem: wanting to go somewhere, but not knowing what was happening nearby.

The best events are often buried in social posts, newsletters, flyers, and word of mouth. Finding them takes too much searching, and sometimes you hear about them after they've already happened.

Comuna brings local markets, dinners, workshops, live music, pop-ups, and neighborhood gatherings into one place. Not everything across Los Angeles. Just the things close enough and timely enough to become a real plan.

We're starting in Santa Monica and Silver Lake + Echo Park, with plans to expand across the city in the near future. By focusing locally, we can highlight the independent places and smaller gatherings that citywide guides often miss.

Sometimes the best plan is a few blocks away.

Because experiencing Los Angeles shouldn't always require weeks of planning or an hour in traffic.

We're gonna help you find them.

How Comuna works

We do the research, so you don't have to.

We collect and source local events, organize them by neighborhood and timing, and surface the plans that feel most relevant right now.

01

Relevant

No endless directory. No list of everything happening across Los Angeles. We focus on experiences people might genuinely want to attend: markets, dinners, workshops, live music, pop-ups, cultural events, and neighborhood gatherings.

02

Nearby

Los Angeles is experienced neighborhood by neighborhood. Comuna helps you discover what is happening close to home, so a spontaneous plan doesn't become an hour in traffic.

03

Now

Good plans don't always need weeks of preparation. Browse what is happening tonight, this weekend, or in the days ahead. Find something that fits. Make the plan. Go.

We look for events that are timely, local, and open to the community. We prioritize independent businesses, local organizers, recurring gatherings, and experiences that give people a reason to engage with their neighborhood.
