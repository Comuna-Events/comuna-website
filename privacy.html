Comuna ("we", "us") helps you find events and places near you in Los Angeles. This policy explains what information the Comuna app and website collect, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, so it's short.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, which you add to your passport when you sign up. It may appear to other users on features like the monthly leaderboard.
  • Feedback and messages you send us in the app or through forms on this website, including your name, email address and anything you write.
  • Host applications, including your business name, contact details and information about your events.
  • Events you submit for the map.

Information from using Comuna

  • Account identifier. When you start using the app we create an account for you with a random ID. We don't require an email address or password to use the beta.
  • Activity, such as events you view, save, RSVP to, share or get directions to, and the points, stamps and patches you earn.
  • Location. If you allow it, we use your device's location to show events near you and to confirm check-ins. When you check in, we store how far you were from the event. We don't track your location in the background.
  • Neighborhood you choose, and app settings saved on your device.
  • Technical information like your device type, browser and IP address, which our service providers receive when your device connects to them.

How we use it

  • To run Comuna: show nearby plans, pick your daily top 4, keep your passport, and verify check-ins.
  • To show hosts aggregate results, such as the number of verified check-ins at their events. [Confirm whether hosts ever see individual names.]
  • To reply to your messages and host applications.
  • To improve Comuna, fix bugs and prevent abuse.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run Comuna. They process information only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: our database, accounts and hosting for app data.
  • Mapbox: maps. Mapbox receives your IP address and the map area you're viewing.
  • Google: fonts on our app and website, and the spreadsheet we use to manage event listings. No personal information is stored in that spreadsheet.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

We may also share information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your account information and activity within [30] days, except where we need to keep it to comply with the law.

Your choices

  • Location: you can turn location access off at any time in your device settings. Comuna still works; you'll just pick your neighborhood by hand.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask for a copy of your information or ask us to correct or delete it by contacting us at [email]. [Once the iPhone app launches, add: "You can also delete your account in the app under Profile."]
  • California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. Contact us to make a request.

Children

Comuna isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use access controls and encryption in transit to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Changes

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update this page and the date above, and let you know in the app when it matters.
