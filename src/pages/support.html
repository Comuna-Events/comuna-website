Support

How can we help?

Quick answers below. If you're stuck, send us a note and a real person will get back to you.

Where does Comuna work?

We're starting in Silver Lake, Echo Park and Santa Monica, with more Los Angeles neighborhoods coming soon.

How do the daily picks work?

Every day Comuna picks four plans close to you: a Headliner, a Staple, a Hidden Gem and a Pop-up. New picks arrive each day.

Why does Comuna ask for my location?

To show what's close to you and to confirm check-ins when you arrive at an event. It's optional; you can choose your neighborhood by hand instead. You can change location access any time in your phone's settings.

My check-in didn't work.

Check-ins need you to be at the event, so make sure location is on and try again once you're there. Still not working? Message us with the event name and we'll sort it out.

How do stamps, patches and points work?

Checking in earns points and a stamp for that neighborhood. Patches are badges for milestones, like your first stamp or visiting three neighborhoods. They're just for fun and have no cash value.

How do I add my event or business?

Head to For Hosts and tell us about it. We add hosts by hand while we're in beta.
